Story mode

Where things go to hide

Toplay is a small arcade of browser games that you play with your eyes and your head. No downloads, no accounts, just tap and play.

The first room, The Toy Room, is a playroom after a very busy afternoon. Every object you find is an everyday English word, so a few minutes of play leave you with twenty words you will not forget.
